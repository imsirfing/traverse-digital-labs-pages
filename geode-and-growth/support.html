Support — Geode & Growth

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email support@traverse.llc and we'll get back to you.

Restoring your purchase

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Settings inside Geode & Growth and tap Restore Purchases to bring back your offline unlock. You'll need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with.

My terrarium isn't growing

The free version grows your terrarium whenever you open the app while connected to the internet. If you're offline and haven't purchased the unlock, growth pauses until you reconnect — it isn't lost, just paused. The offline unlock removes this requirement entirely.
