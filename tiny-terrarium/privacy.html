Privacy Policy — Tiny Terrarium

Last updated: October 2026

Tiny Terrarium is made by Traverse Digital Labs, LLC. This policy is short because the app genuinely doesn't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. Tiny Terrarium has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The app does not transmit any data to us or to anyone else. The only use of your network connection is a local check for whether your device has internet access at all — no data is sent as part of that check, and it's skipped entirely once you've purchased the offline unlock.

In-app purchases

The one-time "offline unlock" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit, Apple's in-app purchase system. We never see or handle your payment information. See Apple's own privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Data stored on your device

Your terrarium's growth progress is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, that progress is gone — there is no account or cloud backup to recover it from, by design.

Children's privacy

Tiny Terrarium does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.
